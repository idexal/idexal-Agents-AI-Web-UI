# Code of Conduct — idexla Agents AI Web UI

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **idexla Agents AI Web UI** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes, learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just the individual

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and taking appropriate, fair corrective action to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions not aligned to this Code, and to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors deemed inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies within all community spaces (GitHub, Discussions, Issues, social channels) and when an individual represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

- ✉️ [info@idexal.com](mailto:info@idexal.com)
- ✉️ [team@idexal.com](mailto:team@idexal.com)

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, respecting privacy and security of the reporter. Enforcement decisions will be communicated transparently where appropriate.

Leaders who do not follow or enforce the Code in good faith may face repercussions as determined by other leaders.
